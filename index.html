Pocket Bibi Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

In short: Pocket Bibi does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information. Everything you do in the game is saved only on your own device.

Who we are

Pocket Bibi is a virtual pet game for iPhone made by Francoise Blanchard (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Pocket Bibi.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Pocket Bibi has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or tracking. The game does not connect to the internet to send or receive your information.

Information stored on your device

To remember your game between sessions, Pocket Bibi saves the following only on your device:

  • Your game progress, such as your Bibi’s stage, hunger, happiness, coins and collected items.
  • The name you choose for your Bibi.
  • Game settings, such as sound and music volume.

This information never leaves your device and is not available to us. You can delete it at any time by starting a new game or by deleting the app.

Device permissions

Pocket Bibi does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Third-party services

Pocket Bibi is built with the Unity game engine and is distributed through Apple’s App Store. We do not add any third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services to the game. Apple may collect information about app downloads and crashes according to your device settings and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children’s privacy

Pocket Bibi is suitable for players of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.

Contact us
