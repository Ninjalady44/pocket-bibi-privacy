Frequently asked questions

How do I take care of my Bibi?

Keep its hunger and happiness up by feeding it meals and snacks, clean up after it, give it a bath when it gets dirty, and put it to bed at night. A Bibi that is well cared for grows up happy.

When does my Bibi grow up?

Your Bibi grows to its next stage once a day, when it wakes up at 7 am. The kind of Bibi it becomes depends on how well it was looked after.

My Bibi is angry or sad. What should I do?

An angry Bibi is hungry, unhappy or dirty. Feed it, play with it or clean it. A Bibi with a rain cloud over its head has run out of food or happiness, so feed it right away.

My Bibi is sick.

Give it medicine as soon as you can. A Bibi left sick for too long can pass away.

Does time pass when the app is closed?

Yes. Pocket Bibi follows your phone’s clock, so your Bibi gets hungry and grows even while the app is closed. If you need to be away, you can leave it with the babysitter, which pauses the game.

How do I earn coins?

Get a job, trade fish, veggies and treasures, and play the mini games. Spend your coins on food, snacks and toys.

How do I start over with a new egg?

Open the clock to reach the status page and choose New Egg.

I lost my progress.

Your game is saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes the save, and it cannot be recovered. We do not store any copy of your game.
